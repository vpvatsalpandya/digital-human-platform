# A-11 · Go-To-Market Plan

## 1. Positioning
"The digital human that runs on your students' phones, built with your faculty, and examined
the way you examine." Category: **medical education platform**, not "3D anatomy app".

## 2. Phase 0 (months 0–4): design partners
- Recruit 3 design-partner institutions in India (one government medical college, one private
  medical college, one nursing/allied group). Terms: free Pro for 12 months in exchange for
  content co-creation (histology scanning, radiology cases), weekly feedback, and a reference.
- Deliverables: named faculty champions, signed content co-ownership agreement, pilot success
  criteria (activation > 50%, one full spotter run, faculty NPS > 40).

## 3. Phase 1 (months 4–12): India launch
- Sales: 2 AEs + founder-led; target 40 qualified medical/dental colleges, close 12–15.
- Channels: anatomy society conferences (ASI, national CME events), NMC-reform workshops,
  dean networks, LinkedIn faculty content, Z-Anatomy/open-atlas community goodwill.
- B2C: NEET/MBBS exam-prep app on Play Store (PWA + TWA), free tier with atlas + limited
  quiz; paid tier with spotters and tutor. Drives inbound institutional interest.
- Proof assets: performance benchmark video on a ₹12k phone vs incumbents; error-report SLA;
  citation policy page.

## 4. Phase 2 (months 12–24): regional expansion and depth
- Resellers in SEA/MEA/Africa; localisation (Arabic RTL, Bahasa, French for francophone
  Africa, Portuguese); price band B.
- Product: OSCE, LTI 1.3 certified, SAML, admin benchmarking, commissioned model set v1.
- Marketing: published outcomes study with design partners (pre/post spotter performance),
  submitted to a medical-education journal.

## 5. Phase 3 (months 24–36): UK/EU/ANZ and US nursing
- Procurement frameworks (UK: G-Cloud; ANZ: panel contracts), GDPR residency in EU region,
  SOC 2 Type II, VPAT.
- US entry through nursing, PA, physiotherapy programmes; partner with an LMS-integrated
  courseware distributor.

## 6. Sales motion
- Land: department licence or 90-day pilot. Expand: institution-wide Pro → Enterprise
  white-label → university group.
- Pricing pages public for Starter/Pro in band A; quote-based elsewhere.
- Objection handling kit: "cadaver replacement?" (no — complement, with a study), "content
  accuracy?" (citations + review + flag SLA), "mobile?" (live benchmark), "data?" (residency,
  DPA, RLS whitepaper).

## 7. Metrics by phase
| Phase | Leading | Lagging |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Weekly active faculty at partners; records reviewed/week | Pilot success criteria met |
| 1 | Qualified pipeline; demo→pilot rate; B2C installs | 15 institutions; USD 0.4M ARR |
| 2 | Reseller-sourced pipeline; LTI installs | 80 institutions; NRR > 110% |
| 3 | Framework listings; enterprise pilots | 250 institutions; USD 9M ARR |

## 8. Budget (Y1, USD)
Sales & marketing 350k; content 350k; engineering 900k; infra 60k; legal/compliance 80k.
