### 3.1 Skeleton

- Reference: 206 named bones in the adult skeleton (standard count). The expected list built in the audit script has 208 entries: the 206 count treats the sternum as one bone, the list splits it into manubrium, body and xiphoid process (+2). The 206 includes the six ear ossicles.
- Present in the male pack: **208 of 208** expected bones (218 bone objects; the extra 10 are 2 foot sesamoid groups, 6 ethmoid air-cell objects and 2 sinus objects). Female: **208 of 208** (218 bone objects).
- Missing: none of the 206.
- Not modelled as separate bones: the patellar/hand sesamoids other than the foot groups; the fused sacral and coccygeal segments (sacrum and coccyx are single bones, as in the count); accessory ossicles and sutural bones (variants, not part of the 206).
- Teeth: 28 real teeth of the 32 permanent teeth. The four third molars are not in any source scan; they are added as **generated schematic** copies of the second molars (section 3.7) and are not counted as real.
- Cartilages: 31 (costal, laryngeal, nasal, articular and others present in Z-Anatomy).
- Joints, capsules and discs: ligaments 298, capsules 32, intervertebral and other discs 73.

### 3.2 Muscular system, tendons, fascia, bursae

- Muscles: 479 (male) / 479 (female), tendons 31, tendon sheaths 38, bursae 78, fascia 51, membranes 12.
- Reference: textbooks commonly quote 600–650 skeletal muscles depending on how they are split; there is no single agreed total, so no percentage is claimed.
- Muscle attachment markers in Z-Anatomy (703 male objects) are annotations, not structures, and are excluded.

### 3.3 Vessels

- Arteries: 440 male / 437 female. Veins: 254 / 253. Lymph nodes: 158 / 158, lymphoid organs 4, ducts 14.
- Named trunk vessels are present (ascending, thoracic and abdominal aorta, superior and inferior vena cava, the cerebral, coronary, limb and visceral branches Z-Anatomy names). The great vessels of the heart are in the core pack from the HRA.
- No agreed reference total for named vessels exists, so none is claimed. Named vessels that Z-Anatomy does not model (many small muscular, cutaneous and perforating branches, most named venous tributaries at the fourth order and below) are missing.
- Capillaries, microvasculature and lymphatic capillaries are out of scope for a gross-anatomy model.

### 3.4 Nervous system

- Nerves 237, plexuses 22, ganglia 4, meninges 2; brain regions 282 (Allen Human Reference Atlas, via HRA).
- **All 12 cranial nerve pairs are present** (I–XII, left and right), checked by the test suite.
- Reference: 31 pairs of spinal nerves. The scans provide the anterior and posterior roots, the cauda equina, the intercostal nerves, the sympathetic trunk, the brachial plexus (roots, trunks, divisions, cords) and the named peripheral nerves of the limbs. **No scan provides the 31 individually named pairs**, so C1–C8, T1–T12, L1–L5, S1–S5 and Co1 (both sides) are supplied as generated schematic tubes (section 3.7), together with the cervical, lumbar and sacral plexuses and the autonomic plexuses. Cutaneous and small nerves of the head, neck and limbs, the cranial ganglia and cardiac conduction tissue are added as schematic stand-ins (section 3.7). Still missing: most other cutaneous branches, dermatomal rami as separate objects, and the fine autonomic plexuses on individual organs.
- Brain and spinal-cord internal structures from Z-Anatomy (239 male objects) are **excluded**: their provenance inside Z-Anatomy is UW Brainder, which could not be verified. The brain is shown from the HRA/Allen regions instead.
- The spinal cord is the HRA spinal cord (real), replacing the earlier stand-in.

### 3.5 Organs, glands, other systems

- Organ parts from the HRA and Z-Anatomy: digestive 39, urinary 79, reproductive 25 (male) / 34 (female), heart parts 10, valves 5, liver parts 18, bronchi 28, larynx 8, eye structures 61, glands 14, endocrine 8.
- Coronary branches, cardiac veins, hepatic/portal branches and rectal, sigmoid and colic vessels that Z-Anatomy lacks come from the HRA 3D Reference Object Library (CC BY 4.0) through an allow-list.
- Lungs and thyroid are now real meshes (Z-Anatomy lobes; the thyroid gland).
- **Inner ear and ear canal are now real** (26 structures per body): scala tympani, scala vestibuli, round window and external acoustic meatus from **OpenEar** (CC BY 4.0, Zenodo 1473724, specimen ALPHA), and cochlear duct, three semicircular ducts, three ampullae, utricle and saccule from **IE-Map** (CC BY 4.0, Zenodo 10625570). They are registered onto each body's real ossicles (ossicle RMS about 0.5-0.6 mm, size kept). The labyrinth is a template from another subject fitted with about 1 mm residual, and the left ear is the right ear mirrored, not a separate scan.
- Still excluded: the Z-Anatomy **inner ear** (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, Univ. of Dundee) and the **Z-Anatomy kidney** (CC BY-NC 4.0). The HRA kidney (CC BY 4.0) is used instead.
- Heart chambers and valves from Z-Anatomy are skipped so that everything registers to the HRA heart in the core pack.
- Mammary gland (female) is the HRA mammary model. The thoracic duct is a schematic stand-in.

## 5. Fit quality (read before calling this "accurate")

- The core HRA organs are in one reference frame, so they line up. The Z-Anatomy geometry comes from a different (male) subject and is registered into each HRA body by a global scale and translation, a spine-following displacement, and a per-leg similarity transform (`scripts/assets/extract/register.py`). Residuals: spine ≈ 4 mm RMS; ossicles/inner ear ≈ 0.5-1 mm (separate registration onto the ossicles); leg landmarks 24–40 mm (male), 8–18 mm (female).
- The **female** body uses the male-derived Z-Anatomy skeleton, muscles, nerves and vessels fitted to the female HRA frame. Sex-specific organs come from the female HRA.
- Fine structures (small nerves, digital vessels, the inner ear) may not sit exactly inside the soft tissue of the fitted body. This has been checked visually only in software-rendered headless Chrome, not by an anatomist.
