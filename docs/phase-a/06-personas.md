# A-06 · User Personas

Each persona lists device, context, jobs-to-be-done, pains, and what "success" looks like.
Personas drive audience modes (Phase B) and UI defaults (Phase F).

## P1 · Priya, first-year MBBS student (India, Tier-2 city)
- Device: ₹14,000 Android, 4 GB RAM, 4G with patchy campus Wi-Fi; shares a laptop.
- Context: 9 hours a day of lectures and dissection; spotter exams every 6 weeks.
- Jobs: revise structures seen in dissection the same evening; practise spotters; understand
  relations and clinical correlations for viva.
- Pains: apps that need 3 GB downloads; content in US-centric terminology; no link to her
  college's exam pattern.
- Success: opens a saved view of the brachial plexus in < 5 s, runs a 20-station spotter,
  sees which regions are weak.

## P2 · Arjun, NEET aspirant (grade 12)
- Device: parent's Android phone in the evenings.
- Jobs: visualise NCERT biology chapters (human physiology, structural organisation).
- Pains: text-only prep; no way to "see" nephron or heart valves.
- Success: school mode with NCERT-aligned vocabulary; short simulations; quiz streaks.

## P3 · Sister Mary, BSc Nursing student
- Device: mid-range Android; low data plan.
- Jobs: applied anatomy for procedures (IM injection sites, catheterisation landmarks).
- Success: nursing mode surfaces surface anatomy and procedure relevance first.

## P4 · Dr. Meenakshi, Associate Professor of Anatomy
- Device: laptop + tablet in the dissection hall.
- Jobs: build a lesson for tomorrow's practical; create a 30-station spotter for 150 students
  and get it graded; see which students are struggling; verify content accuracy.
- Pains: existing tools have no authoring; spotters take three days to set up physically.
- Success: authors a spotter in 20 minutes reusing item bank; analytics by 6 pm the same day;
  can flag and correct a record and see it re-reviewed.

## P5 · Dr. Rahul, PG resident (MD Radiology / MS General Surgery)
- Jobs: cross-sectional anatomy, surgical planes, radiological correlation, teaching juniors.
- Success: selects a structure and immediately sees it on CT/MRI; postgraduate mode gives
  surgical relevance and references.

## P6 · Prof. Sharma, Dean / Head of Department (buyer)
- Jobs: satisfy NMC inspection evidence, improve pass rates, justify budget.
- Success: admin dashboard with seat utilisation, cohort mastery, benchmarking; SSO with
  college ERP; invoice in INR; white-label with college crest.

## P7 · Anita, IT administrator
- Jobs: SSO, roster import, data residency, accessibility compliance, uptime.
- Success: SAML/OIDC in an afternoon; CSV/LTI roster sync; audit logs; DPA signed.

## P8 · Faculty content reviewer (Phase E workflow)
- Jobs: review records queue, request changes, approve; see citations inline.
- Success: reviews 15 records/hour with side-by-side source view.

## P9 · Physiotherapy / Dental / Pharmacy student
- Shares P1's device reality; wants mode-specific emphasis (musculoskeletal for physio, head
  and neck for dental, drug targets and receptor physiology for pharmacy).
